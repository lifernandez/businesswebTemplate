E-Bike Controller Thermal Performance

Industry: Micromobility

Challenge

Controller overheating due to solar loading was causing performance degradation and customer concerns.


Solution

We used Ansys Icepak for solar and thermal simulation, optimizing airflow and material selection.


Results

Hotspot temperature dropped by 14°C

Improved summer reliability

Cost avoided from unnecessary hardware changes

Phone Case Drop Simulation

Industry: Consumer Accessories

Challenge

Reduce the time and cost of physical drop testing.


Solution

Simulated 3 heights with 2 materials using ANSYS Explicit Dynamics.


Results

Reduced prototyping cycles by 60%
